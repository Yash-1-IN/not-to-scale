Not to Scale

This is a hydrogen atom, not to scale of course

Remember, not to scale. If the proton were the size of a marble, the electron would be about half a kilometre away.

Also, protons aren't really red. Colour is a light thing, and a proton is far smaller than the wavelength of light.

Actually, electrons don't orbit like little planets. We only know where one is likely to be, so it's more like a fuzzy cloud.

Denser dots mean more likely. This cloud is called an orbital. More on that later.

Zoom out far enough and the atom disappears. Next to a person, it's too small to draw.

About 0.1 nanometres wide, roughly a billion times narrower than a hand. The arrow is doing all the work.
